## Trust and data model

1. A membership configures the central IR account, team contacts, communication preferences, triage feature and organization/OU coverage.
2. Membership teammates are automatically added to cases and receive email notifications, but do not receive IAM/API permission merely by being contacts.
3. Cases contain current scope/IOCs/watchers/status plus comments, historical edits, investigation actions and attachment metadata.
4. `ListInvestigations` returns rich investigation action content (Evidence, Investigation or Summarization), status and feedback.
5. Attachment upload/download APIs return S3 presigned bearer URLs, separating URL minting from unauthenticated object transfer.
6. Every Security IR API/console call is documented as a CloudTrail management event under `security-ir.amazonaws.com`.
7. AWS-supported cases cannot switch back to self-managed. Premature `CloseCase` asks AWS engineers to stop, and closed cases cannot reopen.

## High-value expected techniques promoted

1. Exact-case read of IOCs, impacted scope, pending action, responder contacts, comments, edit history and investigation recommendations.
2. Exact-case download URL minting to steal forensic evidence without S3 permission.
3. Upload URL minting and attachment injection to poison or distract the evidence workflow.
4. IOC/scope/watcher deletion, case/comment rewrite, misleading feedback, workflow/status manipulation and irreversible closure.
5. Membership team replacement with external attacker emails for notifications on future cases across configured coverage.
6. Single-case watcher addition for narrower passive notification persistence.
7. Unauthenticated use of a leaked presigned attachment URL.
8. Known-candidate organization/membership relationship classification through batch member details.
9. Proactive-triage and OU-coverage impairment through membership update.
10. Immediate membership/investigation destruction through cancellation or delegated-administrator removal.

## Boundary re-audit — 2026-09-29

The account still had no membership in either allowed Region and no paid fixture was created. The current API/authorization model, IAM simulation and safe failed-call telemetry produced these additional public results:

1. `BatchGetMemberAccountDetails` on one exact membership ARN classifies up to 100 supplied account IDs as organization/unrelated and associated/disassociated/unassociated. It does not need Organizations, membership read or list permission. The five-request/second quota makes this a 500-known-candidate/second relationship oracle, not arbitrary account enumeration. CloudTrail records the membership and all candidates in a read management event; its response is `null`.
2. `UpdateMembership` can disable proactive triage or remove selected OU coverage. This stops Security IR ingestion/triage/escalation, not GuardDuty or Security Hub themselves. This is a documented-model result because no paid membership existed.
3. Current cancellation documentation is stronger than the earlier summary: `CancelMembership` immediately deletes the membership, terminates active/ready-to-close resources and investigations, removes historical-case access, and does not restore old cases after later enrollment. Every configured teammate is emailed. No destructive call was made.
4. In a delegated deployment, Organizations management-account `DeregisterDelegatedAdministrator` for `security-ir.amazonaws.com` immediately terminates the membership. `DisableAWSServiceAccess` also severs trusted organization access.
5. All cases remain centrally stored in the membership account. Naming a member as impacted or an email as watcher/teammate grants no API access. With no Security IR resource policies, direct cross-account access requires assuming a role in the central account.
6. Attachment upload/download remains an exact-case, no-S3-permission bearer-URL technique, but poisoning is scoped to the self-managed customer workflow: AWS says its engineers cannot view attachments in AWS-supported cases. Files are limited to 100 MiB and deleted seven days after closure.

### CloudTrail correction

Do not claim that CloudTrail exposes watcher/team email domains, sensitive case text or a returned presigned URL. The service model marks case title/description, comments, responder/watchers, membership name, threat-actor IP/user agent, attachment filename and returned URL sensitive. Live events redacted tested `UpdateCase.title` and `UpdateCaseComment.body` to `***`; read events had `responseElements: null`. Detection should use event/caller/source, case or membership ID, visible scope/status/opt-in values, inventory diffs and mail telemetry.

### Role/confused-deputy review

AWS's containment template trusts `containment.security-ir.amazonaws.com` with the account ID as `sts:ExternalId`, then delegates only the published containment runbooks to an SSM execution role. The EC2-triage variant also grants `ssm:SendCommand` on `*`. A customer identity with ordinary SSM Automation plus PassRole can exercise the known SSM boundary, but no Security IR bypass was found. Whether a different, explicitly authorized Security IR tenant could induce cross-tenant assumption of a predictably configured containment role remains a private fixture-dependent hypothesis, not a finding or public technique.

### Rejected boundaries

- Unsigned native calls require authentication; synthetic known IDs remained authorization-denied.
- Impacted member accounts, watchers and teammates gain no IAM permission.
- Read responses do not place presigned URLs in CloudTrail.
- No direct cross-account resource-policy path exists.
- Arbitrary impacted-account and cross-tenant containment theories were not testable without a second explicitly authorized paid membership and were not promoted.

Revalidation created or modified no memberships, cases, attachments, IAM roles, service-linked roles, containment roles, Organizations settings or other infrastructure.
