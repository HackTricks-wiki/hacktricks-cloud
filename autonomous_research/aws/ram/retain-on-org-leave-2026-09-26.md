# RAM retained shares after organization departure, 2026-09-26

AWS [introduced](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/02/aws-resource-access-manager/) `retainSharingOnAccountLeaveOrganization` in February 2026. [RAM documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ram/latest/userguide/getting-started-sharing.html) says it retains account-to-account access after the consumer account leaves an organization, provided the share was created with `allowExternalPrincipals=true` and the consumer accepts an invitation. The setting is create-only, does not apply to OU or organization shares, and excludes resource types shareable only within an organization. This is a useful variant of existing cross-account RAM persistence, not a new independent technique.

A no-resource/no-principal share was created in `us-east-1` in account `228478051196` with `--resource-share-configuration retainSharingOnAccountLeaveOrganization=true` and `--allow-external-principals`. `CreateResourceShare` returned an ACTIVE share and `GetResourceShares` returned `resourceShareConfiguration.retainSharingOnAccountLeaveOrganization: true`. `DeleteResourceShare` returned `returnValue: true`. No resource or principal was added and no invitation was sent. Account departure and invitation acceptance were not tested; the persistence effect is grounded in AWS documentation. There is no remaining test share.
